Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants convert light energy into chemical energy.

Plants use sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water to produce glucose and oxygen.

Chlorophyll is the green pigment that absorbs light energy during photosynthesis.

Photosynthesis mainly takes place in the chloroplasts of plant cells.

The two main stages of photosynthesis are the light-dependent reactions and the Calvin cycle.

The light-dependent reactions capture light energy and produce ATP and NADPH.

The Calvin cycle uses carbon dioxide, ATP, and NADPH to produce glucose.
